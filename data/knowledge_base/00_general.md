# About the Science Education Institute

*This section introduces the Department of Science and Technology - Science Education Institute (DOST-SEI), its mandate, objectives, history, and the scholarship programs it administers.*

***Source: https://sei.dost.gov.ph/***

***Last Updated: August 2026***

Executive Order No. 128 (January 30, 1987), other wise known as the Reorganization Act of the National Science and Technology Authority, created the Science Education Institute.

## MANDATES *(Per EO 128)*
1. Undertake science education and training
2. Administer scholarships, awards and grants
3. Undertake science and technology manpower development
4. Formulate plans and establish programs and projects for the promotion and development of science and technology education and training in coordination with DepEd, CHED and other institutions of learning

## VISION
Building the next generation of S&T professionals committed to the well-being of every Filipino

## MISSION
To develop the country's S&T human resource and improve the quality of science education through responsive undergraduate and graduate scholarships, innovative science education programs, and the creative promotion of S&T culture

## CORE VALUES
* ### DATA-DRIVEN
  - We prioritize using data to guide our decisions, strategies, and operations. By emphasizing both quantitative and qualitative data, we ensure insights shape our direction, performance, and innovation. We actively foster a culture of data-driven decision-making at all levels, optimizing efficiency and enhancing outcomes for scholars, teachers, students, and other stakeholders. 
* ### LASER-FOCUSED
  - DOST-SEI remains focused on clear goals and objectives, prioritizing efforts that deliver the most impactful outcomes for scholars, teachers, students, and other stakeholders. We embrace precision and clarity, driven by a strong determination to achieve our targets. 
* ### PEOPLE-CENTERED
  - DOST-SEI prioritizes the needs, well-being, and experiences of individuals—whether scholars, teachers, employees, or other stakeholders. We design our decisions, actions, and policies to be responsive to the interests and concerns of those we serve, ensuring their perspectives and values are at the heart of everything we do.

## PROGRAM THRUST
The SEI shall continue to strengthen its capability to develop a critical mass of highly-trained S&T human resource by focusing on the following program thrusts:
1. Expand implementation of S&T undergraduate and graduate programs to achieve varied levels of S&T Innovation sophistication
2. Pursue more vigorously promotion of S&T culture to stimulate interest and prepare adequate numbers of young people to pursue careers in S&T Innovation
3. Develop and implement innovation approaches towards improving the delivery of science education
4. Pursue research activities and develop external linkages in S&T human resource development and science education as part of overall strategy for national policy development, local and external benchmarking and capacity building

##  STRATEGIC GOALS
* Enhance the quality of science education
* Engage in policy stakeholders
* Improce outreach and community engagement
* Achieve organizational excellence
