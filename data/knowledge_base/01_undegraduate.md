# S&T Undergraduate Scholarships

***Source: https://sei.dost.gov.ph/***

***Last Updated: August 2026***

The S&T Undergraduate Scholarships Program aims to stimulate and entice talented Filipino youths to pursue lifetime productive careers in science and technology and ensure a steady, adequate supply of qualified S&T human resources which can steer the country towards national progress.

## Types of Scholarship
*This section explains the different types of DOST-SEI Undergraduate Scholarships, including the Merit Scholarship and the RA 7687 Scholarship Program.*
* ### Merit
  * The DOST-SEI Merit Scholarship Program, formerly known as the NSDB or NSTA Scholarship under RA No. 2067, is awarded to students high aptitude in science and mathematics and are willing to pursue careers in the fields of science and technology. 
* ### RA 7687
  * Republic Act No. 7687, also known as the “Science and Technology Scholarship Act of 1994”, provides scholarships to talented and deserving students whose families’ socio-economic status does not exceed the set cut-off values of certain indicators. Qualifiers must pursue priority fields of study in the basic sciences, engineering, other applied sciences, and science and mathematics teaching.

## Eligibility Criteria
*This section explains who can apply for the DOST-SEI Undergraduate Scholarship. It describes the eligibility requirements, qualifications, and conditions that applicants must satisfy before applying.*
### Grade 12 Students

**Graduating in SY 2026–2027 and past Grade 12 graduates**  
All students/graduates from the STEM class or top 5% from the Non-STEM graduating class.

**Graduating Grade 12 male and female students in SY 2026–2027:**

- **STEM Strand:** All students belonging to the STEM class.
- **Non-STEM Strand:** Must be in the top 5% of the Non-STEM graduating class.

**Graduate of Grade 12 before SY 2026–2027 and has not enrolled in college yet:**

- **STEM Strand:** All students belonging to the STEM class.
- **Non-STEM Strand:** Must be in the top 5% of the Non-STEM graduating class.

---

### Filipino Citizen

Must be a natural-born Filipino citizen.

---

### Character & Health

Must be of good moral character and in good health.

---

### Did Not Qualify

May have applied to the DOST-SEI Undergraduate Scholarship but **did not qualify**.

---

### Did Not Avail

May have qualified for the DOST-SEI Undergraduate Scholarship but **did not avail** of the award.

---

### No College Units

Must **not have earned any post-secondary (college or vocational) units**.

## Application Do's and Don'ts
*This section provides important reminders, guidelines, and best practices when applying for the DOST-SEI Undergraduate Scholarship. It includes instructions on account creation, document submission, email, internet connection, and avoiding common application mistakes.*
### Valid and Secured Email Address

A valid email address is required for logging in to your application account and receiving notifications about your application status and results.

**Do:**
- Ensure that you enter the correct email address when registering.
- Use a secure email account, preferably one with two-step verification enabled.
- Provide an alternate email address in case the registered email cannot be reached.
- Regularly check your email for application updates and announcements.

**Don't:**
- Do not use a school email address because you may lose access to it later and miss important advisories or notifications.
- Do not use an email account that has been compromised in a data breach.

---

### Working Mobile Number/s

Provide an active and working mobile number so DOST-SEI can contact you regarding your application when needed.

---

### Security of Access Credentials

Your **Science Scholarships ID (SSID)** and personal information will serve as your login credentials for the DOST-SEI Undergraduate Scholarship E-Application System.

**Do:**
- Keep your SSID and personal information secure.
- Avoid sharing your access credentials with others.

**Don't:**
- Do not create multiple accounts. This may delay your access to your application forms.

---

### Stable Internet Connection

When completing your E-Application and uploading required documents, make sure you have a stable internet connection to avoid interruptions.

---

### Avoid Websites with Outdated Information

Be cautious of websites that contain outdated or incorrect information about the 2027 Undergraduate Scholarship.

**Important Reminders:**
- All scholarship application transactions are conducted online through the official platform.
- DOST-SEI does **not** request payments or personal information through phone calls, emails, or third-party services.
- Always verify the authenticity of communications and the identity of individuals contacting you before sharing information or making payments.
- Use only the latest scholarship information and official application forms.

The E-Application System has been used for eight years and has undergone several updates. Make sure you are using the latest version of the scholarship forms and the most recent scholarship guidelines.

## Required Documents and Submission Guidelines

### Scholarship Forms C to J

Download the latest Scholarship Forms C to J.

⚠ **Failure to complete the forms properly may result in disqualification from taking the scholarship examination and the opportunity to become a DOST-SEI scholar.** Read and follow the special instructions carefully.

### Instructions for Accomplishing and Uploading Forms

1. Print each form on **A4 size bond paper**.
2. Ensure that each form is properly accomplished and fully signed by the authorized signatory. Missing information may result in disqualification.
3. If submitting a document other than the prescribed form, ensure that it contains all required information stated in the scholarship application form. Missing details (e.g., total number of graduating students in Form E2, years of residency in Form F) **may cause your disqualification**.
4. Scan or take a clear picture of each accomplished form and save the files.
5. Follow the prescribed file naming convention: **(Surname-Form __)**.
6. Upload each form through the corresponding Upload Module in the E-Application System. Submitting an incorrect document may result in disqualification.
7. Ensure that the files you upload are correct, complete, and viewable in the system. Use the preview button in the system to do this. Files that are unviewable will be considered corrupted and will cause your disqualification.

Use the **Preview** button in the system to verify uploaded files. Unviewable or corrupted files will be considered invalid.

### Applicant's Recent Picture

**Required specifications:**

- Passport-size photo:
- **4.5 cm × 3.5 cm**
- **1.8 inches × 1.4 inches**
- Must show the applicant’s face occupying **80% of the picture**.
- Must be colored with a **white background**.
- Must include a handwritten name tag positioned approximately **1 inch (2.54 cm) below the chin** containing:
- Full Name: First Name, Middle Initial, Last Name, and Extension Name (if applicable)
- Example: `JUAN C. DELA CRUZ, JR.`
- Signature over printed name
- Must be taken within the last **three (3) months** before filing the application.
- No filters, heavy makeup, digital enhancements, or alterations are allowed.
- The photo must accurately show the applicant’s natural appearance.

Requirements:

- Ensure that the submitted document is the applicant’s own birth certificate.

## Application Process

### STEP 1  
### Registration

- Create your online application account by clicking the **Register Now** button on the application page and complete all required fields accurately.
- Each applicant **MUST** use a unique valid email address and mobile number that has not been used in any previous registration.
- Only the following email providers are accepted: **Google (Gmail), Outlook, Yahoo, and iCloud.** School email domains (e.g., **edu.ph**) and government email domains (e.g., **gov.ph**) are **NOT** allowed.

#### After the Registration

- You will receive an **Account Activation Email.** Click the activation link to activate your account.
- Once activated, a second email will be sent containing:
  - Science Scholarships ID (SSID)
  - Brief application instructions
- Log in using either your **SSID** or **Email Address**, and birthdate which serve as your verification details.

#### Important Reminders

- Your email address and mobile number will be permanently linked to your account and **CANNOT** be reused for another registration.
- Please double-check all information before submission to avoid login or verification issues.

---

### STEP 2  
### Accomplish Application Form and Upload Documentary Requirements

- Complete all required application modules and upload the necessary documentary requirements.
- You may accomplish the modules in any order and save your progress at any time. A progress bar and status are available on the application dashboard to help you monitor your completion status.

#### Application Modules

- Student Information Questionnaire
- Household Information Questionnaire
- Personal Data
- Family Data
- Contact Information
- School Data

#### Upload Documentary Requirements

- Prepare clear scanned copies or photos of all required documents before uploading them to the system.
- If a document consists of multiple pages (e.g., proof of income), combine all pages into a single PDF file before uploading.

#### File Guidelines

- Accepted file formats: **PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG**
- Each upload accepts one file, with a maximum total size of **5MB** per upload
- Some requirements may have specific size limits as indicated in the system

#### After selecting your files

- Use the **Preview** function to verify that all files are correct and complete
- Click **Upload** once you are satisfied
- You may replace or update files at any time before final submission

#### Important Reminders

- All required modules and documentary requirements must be completed and uploaded on or before **17 September 2026**.
- Uploading incorrect documents and/or not properly accomplished forms is a ground for disqualification.

---

### STEP 3  
### Test Center Preference

- Part of the application process is the selection of your preferred test center for the qualifying examination.
- Your preferred test center will be considered when assigning you to a test center.
- Ensure that you select the correct test center. Review your choice (name of school and location) carefully before proceeding.

#### Important Reminders

- Once you submit your preferred test center, you will **NO LONGER** be able to change it.
- Make sure to carefully consider your likely whereabouts on the examination date before making your selection.
- The list of test centers is tentative and may still change. If your chosen test center becomes unavailable, you will be assigned to the nearest available test center.

---

### STEP 4  
### Final Review and Submission of Application

- Once you have completed all the required application modules and uploaded all documentary requirements, **"Submit"** button will become available at the top of the application dashboard.

#### Before Submission

- Review all application information entered carefully
- Check spelling, accuracy, and completeness of information
- Confirm that all required documents have been uploaded correctly
- Click **Submit** button when everything is in order.

Upon clicking the **Submit** button, you will be prompted to review and agree to the **Terms and Conditions** of the application.

#### Final Reminder

- After submission, **NO** changes can be made to your application.
- Your application will be evaluated based solely on the information and documents submitted.
- Please ensure a thorough review before final submission.

#### After Submission

- A confirmation message will appear on screen indicating successful submission.
- Your application is now officially submitted and will proceed to the verification stage.

## What's next?
*This section explains what happens after submitting an application, including the examination, test permit, announcement of qualifiers, scholarship orientation, and the effectivity of the scholarship award.*
### Issuance of Test Permit

The pen and paper examination is on **November 14-15, 2026**.

The eligible applicant will be issued a Test Permit that indicates the test center, test session, and other pertinent information related to the examination. This will be made available for download by the applicant in the E-Scholarship Application System in **October 2026**.

---

### Announcement of Scholarship Qualifiers

If your performance in the scholarship examination meets the cut-off score, you shall be considered a potential qualifier. You will receive a Notice of Award through your registered e-mail address advising you, among others, the schedule of the orientation on the DOST-SEI Undergraduate Scholarship Policies and the Signing of the Scholarship Agreement.

Check the **SEI website: sei.dost.gov.ph** for the announcements of the scholarship qualifiers, tentatively in **March 2027**.

A qualifier and his/her parent or guardian must attend the Orientation on the S&T Scholarship Policies and sign the Scholarship Agreement to become a DOST-SEI scholar.

---

### Effectivity of the Scholarship Award

The scholarship award is effective for the **First Semester/Term of AY 2027-2028**.
